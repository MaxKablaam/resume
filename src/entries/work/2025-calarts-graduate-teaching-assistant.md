While pursuing my MFA at CalArts, I was given the opportunity to work as a part-time teaching assistant for the Art & Tech program. I delivered a weekly 50-minute tutorial in Scott Benzel's "Pandaemonium Architecture" class on the history of computing and artificial intelligence. Tutorials focused on creative technologies students could incorporate into their art practice, from particle simulations to machine-learning audio synthesis. My responsibilities also included managing the Art & Tech mid-res group show for all students in my cohort.
