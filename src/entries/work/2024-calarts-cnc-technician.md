While pursuing my MFA at CalArts, I worked in the Art Department's Super Shop (Metal + Wood) as a part-time CNC Technician. I was trained to create machine paths from assorted file types and to operate and maintain both the CNC router and laser cutter. I worked with wood materials, including plywood, MDF, and hardwoods, as well as metals such as aluminum and brass.
