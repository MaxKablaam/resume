While pursuing my graduate degree at CalArts, I joined Falsework Studio as Lead Technical Artist for *Spider Lily* (Unreal Engine), bridging the gap between their exceptional artistic vision and technical performance. My work focused on implementing a comprehensive water interaction system, implementing buoyancy physics for both game actors and Niagara particles. Additionally, I developed sophisticated navigation, behaviour, and animation systems for their fish NPC, with State Tree, Perception Components, and the Environment Query System. I was proud to contribute crucial technical architecture, and in return, gaining a glimpse of their exceptional creative process, along with incredible insight into the Los Angeles creative technology community.
